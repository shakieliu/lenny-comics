## Comic 11 — Oliver 👨 (man preset)
**Quote:** "We wanted a company where a thousand people could work, but it'll look like 10 people did it."
**Speaker:** Brian Chesky

### Panel 1
Oliver, [OLIVER_DESC], stands in a massive open-plan office. Hundreds of employees are working at desks stretching into the distance. Oliver looks overwhelmed at the sheer scale. Text bubble: "We have a thousand people now..."

### Panel 2
Oliver sits at his desk late at night, sketching org charts and crossing things out. Papers are scattered everywhere. He draws a circle around "10 people" and writes "THIS" with an arrow. His expression is determined.

### Panel 3
Oliver presents a new streamlined system to the team. The office now has clean, organized pods of people working in sync. Each pod has a clear purpose. Oliver points at a whiteboard that says "One vision. One voice."

### Panel 4
The same massive company, but from the outside it looks like a tiny, perfect workshop. A customer looks at the product and says: "Wow, did like 10 people make this?" Oliver smiles quietly in the background. Bottom text: "We wanted a company where a thousand people could work, but it'll look like 10 people did it." — Brian Chesky.

## Comic 12 — Kenji 👦 (kid preset)
**Quote:** "ChatGPT feels a little bit like MS-DOS. We haven't built Windows yet, and it will be obvious once we do."
**Speaker:** Nick Turley
**Character:** Kenji, a curious elementary student and young inventor. He wears a heavy navy blue button-up cardigan and tan corduroy trousers.

### Panel 1
Kenji, a curious elementary student wearing a heavy navy blue button-up cardigan and tan corduroy trousers, sits in front of an old computer with a green-text MS-DOS command line interface. He squints at the screen and types slowly. Text bubble: "This is... it?"

### Panel 2
Kenji looks at the DOS screen with a puzzled expression. The screen shows a blinking cursor and cryptic commands. He scratches his head. Text bubble: "People use THIS to do stuff?"

### Panel 3
Kenji closes his eyes and imagines a beautiful, colorful, intuitive interface — windows, icons, drag-and-drop, voice commands, holograms. A thought bubble shows his vision of the future glowing with light.

### Panel 4
Kenji opens his eyes, looks back at the DOS screen, and grins with excitement. He cracks his knuckles. Text bubble: "OK. Time to build Windows." Bottom text: "ChatGPT feels a little bit like MS-DOS. We haven't built Windows yet, and it will be obvious once we do." — Nick Turley.

## Comic 13 — Mochi 🐱 (cat preset)
**Quote:** "I have never met this mythical beast called a great natural strategist. Great strategists have all one thing in common, they just practice."
**Speaker:** Roger Martin

### Panel 1
Mochi, [MOCHI_DESC], is lying lazily on a bookshelf in a corporate office. Below, a group of businesspeople are searching frantically for something. One holds a sign: "WANTED: Natural-Born Strategic Genius." Mochi watches with half-closed eyes.

### Panel 2
The businesspeople interview candidates. One candidate claims: "I was BORN a strategist!" Another says: "Strategy is in my DNA!" Mochi yawns from the shelf, unimpressed.

### Panel 3
Mochi hops down from the shelf and walks past a quiet corner where someone is practicing — reading books, doing case studies, running simulations. This person is sweating but focused. Mochi pauses and nods approvingly.

### Panel 4
Mochi sits on the quiet practitioner's desk, looking directly at the viewer with a knowing expression. The "WANTED" poster is crumpled in the trash. Bottom text: "I have never met this mythical beast called a great natural strategist. Great strategists have all one thing in common, they just practice." — Roger Martin.

## Comic 14 — Barnaby 🐶 (dog preset)
**Quote:** "Sell shit, make money."
**Speaker:** Dalton Caldwell

### Panel 1
Barnaby, [BARNABY_DESC], bursts through a door with a box of random products — a lemonade stand sign, hand-made crafts, a "SALE" banner. He's running at full speed with his tongue out, pure excitement. Text bubble: "LET'S GO!"

### Panel 2
Barnaby sets up a scrappy street stand. The sign reads "BARNABY'S STUFF" in messy handwriting. Products are piled everywhere. Other dogs in suits walk by looking confused. One whispers: "Does he even have a business plan?"

### Panel 3
Barnaby is selling enthusiastically. Customers are lining up. Money is flying. He doesn't care about the messy setup — he's just selling and collecting cash with a huge grin.

### Panel 4
Barnaby sits on a pile of money, counting bills with his paws. The suited dogs from earlier are now bankrupt, holding "CLOSED" signs. Barnaby winks at the viewer. Bottom text: "Sell shit, make money." — Dalton Caldwell.

## Comic 15 — Elena 👩 (woman preset)
**Quote:** "There's always this kind of sense that the PM is not the mini CEO. How dare you call yourself that? I actually think that's exactly what the PM is."
**Speaker:** Ben Horowitz

### Panel 1
Elena, [ELENA_DESC], sits in a meeting room. A senior executive points at her dismissively: "You're just a PM. Stay in your lane." Elena's expression is calm but her eyes show fire. Other people at the table look uncomfortable.

### Panel 2
Elena stands up. Behind her, a montage shows everything she does: writing specs, talking to customers, managing engineers, presenting to executives, analyzing data, making roadmap decisions. She's everywhere.

### Panel 3
The same executive watches Elena resolve a crisis — she redirects the engineering team, calms down a frustrated customer on the phone, and updates the board deck simultaneously. His jaw drops. Text bubble: "How is she doing all of that?"

### Panel 4
Elena walks confidently out of the meeting room. Her desk nameplate reads "PM" but someone has taped "= Mini CEO" next to it. She smiles. Bottom text: "There's always this kind of sense that the PM is not the mini CEO. I actually think that's exactly what the PM is." — Ben Horowitz.
